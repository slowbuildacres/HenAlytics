4. **Frost alert notification + Today card**: "Frost alerts before the cold hits"
5. **Seed box**: "Know what seeds you have before you order"
6. **Year-end report**: "Your year in sales and expenses, ready for taxes"
7. **Calendar / planting dates**: "Planting dates for your frost zone"

Tips:
- Caption text goes at the top, 5–7 words, large.
- Show real-looking data, like the garden at $400+, not empty states.
- The first screenshot should be the garden value card. It's the "I want that" moment.
